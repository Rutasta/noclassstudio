WHO IS NO CLASS STUDIO

Brand design, art direction, illustration

NO CLASS STUDIO is a home of one multidisciplinary creative based in Cyprus. My name is Ruta and I work at the intersection of art direction, brand design and illustration — building identities that stand out and are able to tell their own story. With a strong passion for visual and applied arts, I focus on creating thoughtful, strategic design solutions that seamlessly blend concept and aesthetics. I explore unique visual languages, resisting the latest design trends, in favor of intentional design choices that feel both timeless and contemporary.
